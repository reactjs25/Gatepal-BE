GatePal Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 29 December 2025

Last Updated: 29 December 2025

Thank you for choosing GatePal! Your privacy and trust matter to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have about your data when you use the GatePal app, website, and related services (collectively, the “Services”). By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We collect information that identifies you directly or indirectly when you use GatePal. This may include:

  • Registration Details: Name, email address, mobile number, apartment/unit number.
  • Security & Access Data: Visitor or service provider details (name, purpose of visit), delivery information.
  • Account & Communication Data: Messages, support requests, profile images you choose to upload.
  • Financial Information: Payment details when you make payments through the app (collected and processed by trusted payment partners).

We only collect such information when you voluntarily provide it, such as during registration, profile setup, or when interacting with services.

1.2 Automatic and Technical Information

When you use our Services, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique identifiers).
  • Log information (IP address, access times, pages viewed).
  • Cookies and similar technologies to help us operate and improve the Services.

This helps us understand how the Services are used and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Services, including security and access control.
  • Verify your identity and manage your account.
  • Enable visitor approvals and gate operations.
  • Process payments and send transactional alerts.
  • Communicate updates, support responses, and feature information.
  • Improve our Services and personalize your experience.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell or trade your personal information to any third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How We Share Information

We may share your information:

  • With service providers or partners who help deliver the Services (e.g., messaging, cloud hosting, analytics).
  • With your community’s management or authorized representatives if required for security and administrative functions.
  • To comply with legal obligations, or to protect rights, safety, and property.
  • With your explicit consent when you choose to use optional features.

We do not share your personal data with external entities for their own marketing without your permission.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Improve functionality and performance.
  • Understand how the Services are used.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser or device settings. If you refuse cookies, some features may not work as intended.

5. Your Privacy Controls

You have choices about your information:

  • Access & Update: You can review and update your profile information at any time.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of non-essential notifications and promotional messages.
  • Delete Account: You can request deletion of your GatePal account and personal information. We will erase your data unless we are required to retain certain information for legal or safety reasons.

Requests can be sent to support@gatepal.com or through the app.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials (e.g., password). If you suspect your account is compromised, contact us immediately at support@gatepal.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide the Services, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Visitor logs and access records may be kept for a period necessary for operational needs, after which they will be securely deleted.

8. Children’s Privacy

GatePal is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below this age. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you by posting the updated policy on our Services with a revised effective date.
